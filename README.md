# Toolbox for OLX

<img src="assets/icon.png" alt="Toolbox for OLX icon" width="96" align="right">

A Chrome extension that makes OLX easier on the eyes and keeps an eye on the prices you care about:
a soft **dark mode**, a **large photo gallery**, a **price history** for ads in your favourites, and a **cleaner view** without ad banners.

**[Read the full guide: what it does, quick start and settings](https://anleo.github.io/toolbox-for-olx-privacy/)**

![Price changes in lists](assets/1-price-changes-in-lists.png)

## Highlights

- **Dark mode** with a *Black level* slider, *Photo brightness* and a *Theme color*. Photos keep their true colours.
- **Large gallery** with arrow-key navigation.
- **Price history** for favourite ads: a chart on the ad page, a green/red current price with the first price struck through in lists, and price-change alerts.
- **Cleaner view:** hide ad banners and "TOP" ads, choose how many ads per row.
- Interface in Ukrainian, English, Polish, Romanian, Bulgarian and Portuguese.

## Privacy

Everything is stored only in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
Read the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).
